# Accepted-step melt maximum contract

Contract fixed before implementation, 2026-09-16. Numerical extraction only;
no new constitutive physics or experimental validation.

- Observe every accepted thermal step endpoint (not rejected source trials).
- Molten means cell temperature >= liquidus [K] and centre z < the surface
  used for that step. Initial preheat is below solidus. No initial molten state.
- Both backends use uniform Cartesian cubes. Maximize molten cell count,
  equivalent to volume = count * dx^3 [m^3]. Strict increase selects a new peak;
  ties retain the earliest accepted endpoint, without floating volume tie drift.
- Report volume [um^3], scan-axis length/width [um], depth from that step's
  deposited surface [um], and largest global-x cell slice area [um^2], all
  from that same field. Slice area is NOT a scan-normal section for rotated tracks.
- Use the layer active during the step, including endpoints on deposition
  boundaries. Length/width include the projected cube extent.
- Preserve one peak field in memory and write once. Display history stays sparse.
  Peak NPZ includes temperature, active mask, liquid fraction, coordinates,
  surface, angle, time, accepted step, liquidus and mesh spacing.
- Zero melt: zero geometry, null peak time/step, no peak artifact (remove stale
  peak output if an artifact directory is reused).
- Diagnostics identify accepted-step extraction and report peak time/step,
  observed accepted steps, sparse-sample maximum volume and relative missed
  volume (zero when no melt). This loss is playback decimation loss, NOT a
  bound on timestep or mesh error or between-step peaks.
- Reject OpenFOAM executables missing this extraction contract/output.

Independent acceptance fixture: 2 um cubes; sampled endpoint counts 1 and 2,
unsampled counts 3 and 3. Expected maximum 24 um^3 at the FIRST count-3
endpoint; sparse maximum 16 um^3, relative missed volume 1/3. Include a hot
inactive cell, exactly-liquidus cells, mutated source arrays, no-melt reuse,
45-degree projection and a second-layer surface. Reconstruct geometry from
saved fields independently. Compare real backends on single/rotated multilayer
cases; repeat with three maximum timestep limits and record actual steps.
Equal voxel peaks alone do not establish continuum convergence.

## Recorded verification

See `LPBF_PEAK_STUDY_2026-09-16.json`, generated by
`PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 verify_lpbf_peak.py ../docs/LPBF_PEAK_STUDY_2026-09-16.json`.
All ten real backend runs passed independent cube-corner/NPZ checks and paired
geometry agreement at rtol 1e-8, peak temperature agreement within 1%.

IN718 estimated properties; 40 W, 800 mm/s, 40 um grid, 200 um track, 100 us
cooling. The final floating-point endpoint may add one tiny NumPy step; peak
step/time match. Temperature differences decrease approximately twofold.
Constant three-cell peak volume is voxel quantization, not proof of converged
continuum geometry. No mesh refinement or experimental comparison in this study.

Rotated two-layer case (30 W, 35 degree initial angle, 67 degree layer rotation)
has a nonzero maximum in layer two: 192000 um^3 at 451 us, step 455 (604 total).
10 W case has no melt in either backend. Playback loss is zero for these coarse
fixtures; the independent synthetic between-sample fixture establishes the
nonzero-loss case (1/3) without presenting synthetic data as a measurement.
